Manuscripts
01

A Multi-Criteria Analysis Approach to Assess Wildfire Risk to Populated Areas in San Diego County

M.S. Thesis Manuscript · Twyman, M., Stow, D., Jennings, M., Jankowski, P., Paulus, G. · 2025–2026

GIS-MCDA framework combining wildfire hazard, exposure, and social vulnerability submodels to assess and rank wildfire risk across Populated Places and Tribal Reservations in San Diego County, with variance-based sensitivity and uncertainty analysis. Funded by NASA's Equity and Environmental Justice Program (Award 21-EEJ21-0037).

02

Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) for Assessment and Site Selection of Retention Basins for Flood Risk Management in Southern Austria

Research Paper for the Marshall Plan Foundation · Twyman, M. · Carinthia University of Applied Sciences / SDSU · 2025

GIS-MCDA site-suitability framework for decentralized flood retention basins in Carinthia, Austria, with global sensitivity and uncertainty analysis identifying 20 priority candidate sites from 753 initial polygons.

03

Goldspotted Oak Borer Assessment, 2020 — Long Potrero Preserve, San Diego County, California

Client Technical Report · Twyman, M., King, M., Terlep, M. · Prepared for Back Country Land Trust · June 2020

Full-preserve infestation survey and GIS inventory of 899 tagged oak trees, updating a 2018 baseline assessment and delivering tiered treatment recommendations to the land manager, following a modified U.S. Forest Service assessment protocol.
